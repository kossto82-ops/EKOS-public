Applying the multi-agent review harness slice-by-slice across the whole project.

with checkpoint → re-verify (tsc + vitest + browser/DB) → record learnings.
Run one slice at a time (each panel is high token cost; user approves each).

## Rules
- Deliberate deviations & product decisions are recorded, not "fixed" blindly (e.g. retrieval denylist vs allowlist).
- Fixes get their own verification; a panel that fixes may not re-report closure without a re-audit.
- Learnings accumulate in `learnings.md`; the standing brief is `master-prompt.md`.
- User commits locally — do NOT git commit.
